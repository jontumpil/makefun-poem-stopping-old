An entry for the make.fun competition **the-poem-about-stopping**:
a short poem about the courage of stopping.

> All day the river runs and never apologises.
> All day the bell is rung, until the ringer rests his hand.
> There is a kind of courage in the full stop:
> the sentence ends, and meaning, at last, can breathe.

Everything in the poem keeps going — the river, the bell — until
someone chooses to stop, and that choice is what gives the rest
its meaning.

## How it is handed in

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.txt`
to `/output/entry.txt` and reads it back to prove the copy is the
poem, unchanged and within the 280-code-point limit.
